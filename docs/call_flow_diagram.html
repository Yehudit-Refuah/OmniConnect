One call, step by step

The day, at a glance

8:30 AMOmniConnect runs the eCW query and prepares tomorrow's call list (families grouped, arrival times, language).
9:00 AMPBX sends the first GET and starts calling. Each call follows the flow above.
2:00 PMGET again: only calls with no answer or busy are offered for a retry.
6:00 PMLast retry. Unreached calls are marked FAILEDMSG in OmniConnect (not in eCW yet).
Fri / holiday eveNo calls after 12:00 PM. On Shabbat and holidays the GET returns no calls.
Every transfer goes to the call's operator_extension with a short note for the operator · “Cancel now” cancels the visit (CANCPHONE); “cancel only with a new appointment”, reschedule, and cancels on calls with several appointments go to staff and don't change the visit · Times 2:00 PM and 6:00 PM are examples until agreed with the PBX team · Wording is illustrative; the real text comes from the API.
